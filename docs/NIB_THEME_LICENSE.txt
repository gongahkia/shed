Nib palette attribution
=======================

Palette colors for the Nib Dark and Nib Light built-in themes are derived from
https://github.com/gongahkia/nib, specifically dist/nib-palette.json and the
Sublime Text color schemes. Shed's palette mapping is maintained in
src/main/java/shed/ConfigManager.java.
